Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started.

Can I try Nova for free before purchasing?

Yes! We provide a full-featured 14-day free trial on the Professional tier with no credit card required. You can also stay on our generous free Starter plan forever.

How does Nova keep my organization's data secure?

We adhere to strict SOC2 Type II compliance standards. All customer data is encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We never train public foundation models on your private workspace data.

Which third-party tools can I connect?

Nova integrates out of the box with over 100+ platforms including GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Slack, Linear, Google Workspace, and Figma. Custom integrations can also be built using our REST & Webhook APIs.

What happens if our team needs more automation capacity?

You can instantly scale your automation quota with flexible add-ons or upgrade seamlessly to our Enterprise tier which includes dedicated compute and bespoke SLA terms.
